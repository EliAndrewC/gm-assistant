# Feature 219 - the GM's request, verbatim

Dictated 2026-10-18. Substitutions read silently per the user-level transcription table: none needed in this message.

> I would like to create a new money skill. You will be working with the diagram project on this because that project already has a lot of research capabilities, and we would like to essentially, as a goal, figure out what things would realistically cost in our setting instead of just completely hand waving. I realize that there is a lot of difficulty here because pre-modern sources are not always great. I know that, for example, we have literally zero sources saying what a Roman gladius costs. Which in a sense is kind of crazy because people had to buy their own and it was a very important part of the Roman legions. And we have all kinds of records, including records about what things cost, but you know, it just wasn't the kind of thing that it made sense for scribes to copy and preserve over the course of thousands of years, and so now we don't know what one cost. I mean, we can kind of figure it out because we know what other similar things cost, and we know what metal cost, and we know what smithed metal cost, and we know what certain other types of weapons cost, and we know what other equivalent types of swords cost in roughly equivalent societies. but we can't just look up the price of a gladius at any given time during the Roman Empire. Still, as long as we have some amount of internal consistency, then we're in pretty good shape. And we would like to base this on a combination of things, including research that we have done and purchasing power parity calculations and kind of just that should help us figure out what a Koku gets you Although, of course, very few people would buy things with Koku because that is just an absurdly high amount of money. And just as most people did not use gold coins in their day-to-day life, in pre-modern societies, most people in this setting do not either, for the same reason. Player characters who do are exceptionally wealthy in a way which is noteworthy.
>
> Anyway, I have had the diagram repo create a handoff file for you which you can use as the basis for your money skill. It contains information and directions and such. And it is located at /host-l7r-repo/money/HANDOFF.md which is in the same folder as a bunch of old notes and things, and I believe that the digest explains those other old notes and includes a link to our public spreadsheet about purchasing power parity and a different public spreadsheet in which one of my players converted monetary figures from Jane Austen novels to modern dollars and then converted them to koku as an exercise because Jane Austen is writing sort of during the final pre-industrial generation in Britain. I mean, the steam engine had been invented, but you know, Britain had not industrialized at that point, and most workers were not working in modern factories yet, etc.
>
> Anyway, please take a look at that handoff file and then use it as the basis for the new money skill. I will then have you do some back and forth with the diagram project, which has a session running right now. And the two of you will use GitHub to communicate through GitHub issues. And they will be handling the research questions that come up, of which I am sure that there will be a lot. But this seems as good as any a time to get started. Thanks.
