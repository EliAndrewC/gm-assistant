## Data (all under `webapp/l7r/money/data/`)

| path | holds | rule |
|---|---|---|
| `goods.json` | every good: id, name, unit, why it is in or out | one definition per good |
| `baskets/<id>.json` | name, description, `goods: {id: quantity per adult-year}` | `standard` (default), `standard-no-housing`, `bassino-ma-a` |
| `snapshots/YYYY-MM.json` | date, priced_by, source, `goods: {id: [option, ...]}`; an option is `priced_as`, `dollars_per_unit`, `link`; the first is the default, the rest are the substitutes | never edited once committed; `reprice` writes the next |
| `eras/<id>.json` | place, years, confidence, `coins: {unit: {silver_g, source}}`, `goods: {id: {silver_g, label, source}}`, `wage: {silver_g_per_day, source}` | a new era is a new file; `kyoto-1745` is the REFERENCE: Rokugan's prices are its prices (the 2017 sheet's rice anchor) |
| `conversions.json` | GENERATED by `export`: per era and coin, goods dollars, wage dollars and Rokugani sen per unit | a test fails if stale |

The 2017 snapshot file moves from `baskets/2017-04.json` to `snapshots/2017-04.json` in the new shape (its numbers
unchanged; the test pinning $552.39 is the proof), since `baskets/` now means composition.

## Arithmetic (`l7r/money/basket.py`)

- A good counts in a reading when its quantity is above zero and it is priced on both sides (snapshot and era);
  every reading names the goods it left out.
- `ppp_per_koku = dollars(G) / silver_ref(G) x koku_kg x rice_ref` (rice_ref = 1.49 g/kg from the reference era).
- Era conversion of S grams of silver in era E over the goods G priced everywhere: `r = silver_E(G) / silver_ref(G)`;
  goods dollars = `S / r x dollars(G) / silver_ref(G)`; Rokugani sen = `S / r x 1000 / (koku_kg x rice_ref)`; wage
  dollars = `S / wage_E x modern_day` (modern_day = the wage reading's year / 250 days).
- `readings.py` keeps the koku mass, the wage readings and `laborer_days`; `big_deal_per_koku` becomes
  `wage_dollars_per_koku`.

## Commands (`python3 -m l7r.money`)

`convert`, `dollars`, `price`, `history` gain `--basket`; "big deal" becomes "wage dollars" (`--wage-dollars`).
New: `baskets` (every basket side by side), `reprice YYYY-MM`, `crosscheck` (BLS, `l7r/money/bls.py`, a fetch boundary
plus a pure parser tested on a saved response), `era <amount> <unit> --era <id>`, `export`. `check` also validates
baskets, snapshots and eras (an unpriced or unlinked option fails).

## Site

(the playground). Templates `money_index.html`, `money_koku.html`; `static/js/money.js` (pure `compute(data, state)`,
exported for node, plus the page wiring), `static/css/money.css`. The route embeds `playground_json()` and renders the
default state's numbers from the Python twin `playground_reading()`. `tests/test_money_routes.py` checks the pages;
`tests/test_money_js.py` runs `money.js` under node on several states and asserts it matches Python.
