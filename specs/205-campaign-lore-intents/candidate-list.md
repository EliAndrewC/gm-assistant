## I. Named characters

**AMENDED - and this is the correction that matters.** The list as presented ranked
`Akodo no Damasu` (47 mentions), `Bayushi no Daika`, `Matsu no Masao`, `Doji no Tsume`,
`Hida no Reiji` and `Mirumoto no Ryusei` as people. They are not. The GM: *"that is a name prefix.
A member of the Damasu house of the Igodo family might be 'Akodo no Damasu Sei'."* The notes state
it outright: *"Akodo is the family name, 'no Damasu' indicates that he is 'of the Damasu' vassal
house, and Kojima is his personal name."*

So the ranking was measuring HOUSES. Kept as individual categories, being genuinely significant
people:

**Named by the GM** (*"such as the ones that you listed"*): `akodo_damasu` - **which turned out to be
a HOUSE, not a person, and is therefore handled by cluster M instead** - plus `kitsu_okura`,
`soshi_saibankan`, `moto_gaheris` *(in F)*, `grand_abbot_benshi`.

**Added by the implementer** under the "such as" latitude, and marked as such so the list stays
checkable: `akodo_toturi` · `moto_khuyag` · `kuni_yori` · `kuni_isamu`.

Everything else that is merely name-shaped is handled two ways, and they must not be confused:

- **A house or lineage** (`Akodo no Damasu`, `Mirumoto no Ryusei`) routes to the houses handling
  in cluster M - NOT to `samurai_lineages`, which is the concept, and not to the dismissal.
- **An individual with no category** - `Family Given` (`Shinjo Jotsu`), `Family no House Given`
  (`Akodo no Damasu Sei`), or a bare given name, these being examples rather than the whole set - draws
  the dismissal the GM asked for: *"ugh, are you asking me about specific individuals? Come on.
  That guy's a loser. Why are you even bothering? Why do you even care?"*

## M. Famous houses (2)

*"there is probably room for a famous houses section like the Damasu probably deserve their own
entry."*

`famous_houses` *(the notes name the Karo Houses: the Akito of the Hida, the Tsume of the Doji, the
Damasu of the Akodo)* · `damasu`

This is where a bare `Akodo no Damasu` goes - a house, not a person, and not a nobody.

**Scope these two patterns deliberately.** `damasu_domain` (cluster H, the place) and `damasu`
(cluster M, the house) can both be claimed by the bare word "Damasu", and FR-019 puts topics above
houses - so without care the one house the GM asked for by name becomes the hard one to reach. The
domain pattern must require the place sense ("Damasu domain", "Damasu lands", "Damasu provinces");
the bare family-and-house name goes to the house.
