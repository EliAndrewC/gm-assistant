Money

Price sheets

What things cost in Rokugan, in the coins a buyer would hand over: food and lodging, cloth, books, tools, fuel, entertainment, and what work pays.

A koku is 1,000 sen: 10 bu, each of 10 zeni, each of 10 sen. A range is the spread of the evidence or of the guess behind a price, not room to haggle. Each item says how solid it is: researched rests on the historical record, guess is reasoned from neighboring prices and waits for research. Days are an unskilled laborer's days of pay, about 13 sen a day. Dollars are goods dollars (what it buys today), and for wages, wage dollars (how much it matters); What's a koku worth? explains both.
