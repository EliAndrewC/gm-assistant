**Money research: campaign costs III - fruit, salt and small offerings, skilled day wages (Edo first)**

The last of the GM's twelve campaign questions not yet asked (gm-assistant money skill; first answers in
`.claude/skills/money/questions.md`, logged as estimates for calibration under feature 220). Japan-first, China as
comparison, each price beside the same place and period's unskilled day wage.

### 1. Fruit

- **Question**: what did apples (native small apples, *wa-ringo*), persimmons and pears cost by the piece or the
  basket, in season, in Edo and in castle towns?
- **Figure needed**: price per piece or per standard measure.
- **Beside it**: ledger guess: about 1 sen for two to four apples in season.

### 2. Salt

- **Question**: what did salt cost by the *sho* or by weight, retail, in Edo and inland; and in Qing China under the
  salt monopoly (the comparison closest to Rokugan's Imperial salt monopoly)?
- **Figure needed**: price per kg or per *sho*; the monopoly markup.
- **Beside it**: ledger guess: a cup (~250 g) 1-2 sen. Canon: an Imperial salt monopoly covers much of the Empire
  (`setting/l7r.md` "The Ministry of Revenue").

### 3. Hemp cloth, small bags and shrine paper

- **Question**: what did hemp cloth (*asa*) cost against cotton, and what did shrines charge or expect as a donation for
  amulets, talismans and sanctified paper (*ofuda*, *omamori*)?
- **Figure needed**: hemp cloth per *tan*; the customary donation for an amulet.
- **Beside it**: ledger guesses: a small hemp bag 1-2 sen, a sheet of shrine paper about 1, Otsuki's salt bag 3-5 sen.

### 4. Skilled day wages

- **Question**: what did skilled craftsmen (carpenters, tailors, fletchers, smiths) earn per day in Edo and castle
  towns, against unskilled day labor in the same place and period?
- **Figure needed**: the skilled/unskilled ratio, with figures.
- **Beside it**: ledger guess: skilled 20-40 sen a day against unskilled ~13 (1.5-3x).
