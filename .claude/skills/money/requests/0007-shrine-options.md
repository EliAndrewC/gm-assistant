**Money research: the rest of the shrine builder's options (not priority; after #6)**

The gm-assistant shrine builder (feature 216) now takes every price from a money-skill estimate made before research
(estimates logged 2026-10-18 as `shrine catalog <category>/<option>/<contract>`; the method is craftsman-days at 30-50

in Shizu's design; this issue covers the alternatives, so the whole catalog can be graded. Japan-first; each figure beside
the craftsman's and laborer's day wage of the same place and period. Where #6's answer already covers an item (other
roofs, stone posts, rites), say so rather than repeating it.

  accents 1.0, lattice 2.0, relief panels 8.4.
