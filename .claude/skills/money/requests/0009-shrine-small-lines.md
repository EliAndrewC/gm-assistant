estimates logged 2026-10-18 as `shrine catalog <category>/<option>/<contract>`; the method is craftsman-days at 30-50

- **Question**: laborer-days or costs for marking out an open-air sacred space bound with rope (*himorogi*) (S); clearing

### 2. Small carpentry, furnishings and lettering

- **Question**: felling and sawing timber from a shrine's own woods; a stone offering table, setting a flat stone from

### 4. Porterage
