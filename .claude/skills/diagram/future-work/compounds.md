# Future work: compounds (Mode A plans)

**Everything to do with a walled compound drawn as an interior plan** rather than a settlement in its
fields: magistracies today, and - as they are built - samurai city estates, governor's mansions,
samurai country estates, temple precincts drawn in their own right, and keeps.

**This file is EMPTY on purpose, and that is a finding rather than an oversight.** The 2026-08-24
audit of the old single-file backlog turned up no open compound work at all. Every open item was a
settlement item. Two honest readings, and it is worth knowing which is true before the next compound
is drawn:

- the compound plans genuinely are in good shape - they are older, hand-authored, frozen, and have
  had `building-review` and `size-audit` passes against them; or
- nobody has looked at one recently, because all the pressure has been on the scripted hamlet work.

The second is more likely. When the next compound type is started, run `building-review` and
`size-audit` over an existing magistracy FIRST and put what they find here - an empty backlog for a
subsystem nobody has audited is not evidence of health.
